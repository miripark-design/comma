06Dieter Rams · Ten Principles
01
Good design is innovative
Breathing as UI — the interaction itself is the content. The app has no content except the act of using it.
02
Good design makes a product useful
One task. No navigation, no dashboard, no features beyond the core. Every screen is purposeful.
03
Good design is aesthetic
Warm off-whites extracted from physical Braun products. One accent. Restraint in every dimension.
04
Good design makes a product understandable
Three patterns, plain names. No jargon. The box tells you exactly what to do.
05
Good design is unobtrusive
The app disappears. Only the breath remains. No chrome, no navigation, no distraction during a session.
06
Good design is honest
No streaks, no badges, no gamification. The completion message acknowledges the moment without exploiting it.
07
Good design is long-lasting
No trends. The palette could be from 1962. DM Sans reads like Helvetica. Nothing will feel dated in ten years.
08
Good design is thorough
WCAG AA contrast ratios, reduced motion support, keyboard navigation, ARIA live regions. Nothing overlooked.
09
Good design is environmentally friendly
No backend, no tracking, no unnecessary requests. localStorage only. Sound synthesized — no files downloaded.
10
Good design is as little design as possible
Three screens. One accent. Nothing decorative. Every element removed until only the necessary remained.
07Rules of Restraint
One accent per screen. #c8540a appears on exactly one element at a time. Its presence means something.
No shadows. Depth is created by color contrast alone. Nothing floats, nothing casts.
No border-radius on rectangles. Buttons and containers are hard-cornered. Softness comes from color, not geometry.
No gradients. Every surface is a single flat tone from the palette. Nothing blends.
No decorative type. No italics, no display fonts, no ornament. Typography is functional structure, not illustration.
No icons. Words do the work icons would do. Language is precise; symbols introduce ambiguity.
